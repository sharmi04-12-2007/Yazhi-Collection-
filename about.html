About Yazhi Collection

Luxury women's boutique founded on timeless Indian elegance, bespoke tailoring, and personalized service in Coimbatore.

Who We Are

Where Every Woman Shines

Yazhi Collection brings together timeless Indian elegance and contemporary fashion for women who want every outfit to feel special. From bridal and festive wear to sarees, party styles, ethnic collections and customized designs, Yazhi Collection focuses on thoughtful fashion, personalized choices and a memorable boutique experience.

Every garment in our boutique is chosen with purpose. We reject mass-produced uniformity in favor of limited-edition drops, hand-selected fabrics, and custom-made pieces designed to fit each woman's body and occasion with effortless grace.

Our Core Values

The Yazhi Philosophy

Pure Heritage Textiles

We work hand-in-hand with authentic handloom weavers and zardozi artisans to bring pure silks, organic cottons, and genuine tested zari to life.

Personalized Client Care

Shopping at Yazhi is an intimate boutique experience. Sharmila M personally assists clients with styling, sizing, blouse cutouts, and color coordination.

Custom Tailoring

No two women are the same. Our dedicated in-house tailoring unit accommodates custom measurement requests, sleeve modifications, and unique color combinations.
