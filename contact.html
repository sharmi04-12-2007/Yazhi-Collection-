Frequently Asked Questions

How does Yazhi handle custom blouse and lehenga tailoring? +
Every custom order is personally handled by our master cutting masters. You can either enter your measurements directly on your profile page, share a well-fitting sample blouse with our studio, or hop on a 15-minute video call where our stylist guides your measurements step-by-step.
Are the silk sarees authentic and handloom certified? +
Yes! 100% of our pure silk sarees carry the government-recognized Silk Mark certification, woven on traditional pit looms in Kanchipuram, Arani, and Banaras using pure silver-gilt zari.
Can I customize a design in an alternative color? +
Absolutely. Every product card features our customization button ("Love this design in another color?"). Our artisans can dye yarns to your custom Pantone or fabric swatch upon request.
What are the delivery timelines for international orders? +
Ready sarees ship within 24 hours. Custom-stitched outfits and bespoke bridal trousseaus take 5-10 business days for hand-embroidery. We ship internationally via DHL/FedEx Express (3-5 days delivery worldwide).
